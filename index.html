About Me

Board-Ready Hospitality HR & Workforce Governance Leader | Independent Director Aspirant | CHRO | HR Transformation Consultant.

Over three decades, Koteswara Rao has done what few HR leaders have: he has operated not as a support function, but as a boardroom-level strategist — shaping enterprise value, governance culture, and organisational resilience across some of India's and the Middle East's most demanding hospitality environments.

From manufacturing floors to five-star lobbies, from startup hotel groups to the boardrooms of international chains, his career spans 100+ hotels, 6,800+ employees, 32+ nationalities, and multi-country leadership mandates. HR, in his view, is not a cost centre — it is a direct driver of profitability, customer experience, compliance, and long-term enterprise value.
